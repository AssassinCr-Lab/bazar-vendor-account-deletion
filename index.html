Account Deletion Request

You can use this page to request the deletion of your Bazar Vendor account and its associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to our support address using the email subject Bazar Vendor Account Deletion Request.

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your registered email address.
  • Your registered phone number, if applicable.
  • Your store name.
  • A clear statement that you want your account deleted.

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Request Account Deletion

Data that may be deleted

Depending on the data associated with your account and applicable legal requirements, a deletion request may cover your account details, name, store name, email address, phone number, and other personal data associated with your account.

Processing and data retention

Sending a request does not mean that your account has already been deleted. We will review your request and communicate with you about its status. Any applicable data retention requirements will be explained when your request is reviewed.
